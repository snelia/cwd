AliExpress Usability Test

Tester Tasks

1. Buy a navy blue men’s suit jacket for under $10 for a bachelor party

The tester quickly and effectively used the search function and dollar amount filters.

Time: 17.85 seconds

2. Use a coupon code for wireless earbuds that has at least a $1.00 discount

The tester found it extremely difficult to find information about coupons.

Time: unable to complete

3. Find free Hello Kitty jewelry to add to your order for your niece

The tester was able to find a free item outside of the method we intended to test.

Time: 39.70 seconds

4. Find a rice cooker on Walmart and compare its price on AliExpress

The tester visually compared the items instead of using the image search function, but found a comparable rice cooker on AliExpress.

Time: 95.11 seconds

5. Find AliExpress’s policy on returning human hair wigs

The tester quickly found the return policy, so most of the time was spent scrolling the page to find wigs.

Time: 26.10 seconds
